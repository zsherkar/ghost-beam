## Data and Safety Notice

The live Ghost Beam demo uses synthetic accelerator-control data generated locally by the Ghost Beam synthetic JAX digital twin. It does not include real facility eLogs, real accelerator telemetry, live camera feeds, or live EPICS data.

Ghost Beam does not connect to real accelerator hardware and does not perform real hardware writes. EPICS and Archiver references are disabled stubs or future read-only interface shapes.

Public dataset adapters and manifests are references only. No full public dataset is bundled or automatically downloaded, and no rights to any public dataset are granted by this repository.
